Strict Separation of Privileges

Your Master Password and derived keys never sit in the WebView's JavaScript heap. FerriVault derives your 32-byte Master Key via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000+ iterations) or Argon2id, stretches it with HKDF-Expand, and verifies every EncString MAC in constant time.

  • ✓ Automatic Memory Wiping: All key structs derive zeroize::ZeroizeOnDrop. Locking the vault overwrites key bytes in RAM immediately.
  • ✓ Smart Clipboard Hygiene: Copied secrets are hashed and automatically wiped from the OS clipboard after 30 seconds only if untouched.
  • ✓ Offline Unlock & Inactivity Auto-Lock: Unlock your cached encrypted vault locally and configure custom inactivity timeouts.

Everything You Need in a Desktop Vault

Designed for developers, sysadmins, and privacy-conscious teams running Bitwarden Official or self-hosted Vaultwarden.

Zeroize-On-Drop Cryptography

All master keys, stretched HKDF keys, and decrypted buffers use zeroize in Rust so secrets are overwritten in memory the instant the vault locks.

🏢

Organizations & Collections

Switch seamlessly between your Personal Vault and Organization Vaults, assign items to shared Collections, and inspect password change history.

⚡

Keyboard-First Navigation

Trigger global search with Ctrl+K, create items with Ctrl+N, open the generator with Ctrl+G, lock with Ctrl+L, or open settings with Ctrl+,.

🖥️

Native Linux Wayland & Tray

Includes StatusNotifierItem (ksni) tray support on KDE Plasma & GNOME, left-click window toggle, and automatic Wayland .desktop icon registration.

🔐

Built-in TOTP Authenticator

Parses standard otpauth:// URIs and raw Base32 secrets with SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 support and a live countdown indicator.

🌍

1-File Community i18n

Adding a new language requires copying a single JSON dictionary and verifying 100% key parity with make test-i18n.
